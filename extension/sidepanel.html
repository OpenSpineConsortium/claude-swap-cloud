Copy a project to another account

  1. Open the project on claude.ai in this tab, signed in as the account that has it, and press Export this project.
  2. When it says Exported, press Sign out and reload, and sign in as the other account on the page that opens.
  3. Give the copy a name if you want one and press Import: the copy goes into the account you signed in as. The new project's link appears under Progress when it is done.
