cswap project import: store assets

Drawn 2026-10-05 for extension 0.1.0 by src/build_assets.mjs, from
src/frame.html and src/icon.svg, with the real sidepanel.html in the
screenshots and the tiles (fed a stand-in chrome.storage holding the scenes
in src/scenes.mjs; the interruption's texts are interrupt.js's own). The
account and the project in them are made up.

To draw them again (needs Playwright and its Chromium, a maintainer's tool,
not a dependency of the extension):

    node extension/store-assets/src/build_assets.mjs

It also rewrites the package's icons, extension/icons/{16,32,48,128}.png,
from the same icon.svg.

WHAT GOES IN WHICH DASHBOARD FIELD (Store listing tab)

  Store icon (128x128)          store-icon-128.png
  Screenshots (up to 5)         screenshot-01-importing.png, screenshot-02-stops-and-tells.png,
                                screenshot-03-done.png, in that order
  Small promo tile (440x280)    promo-tile-440x280.png
  Marquee promo tile (1400x560) marquee-1400x560.png (optional)

THE FILES

store-icon-128.png (128x128, RGBA)
    The mark: two arrows passing each other on a dark rounded square, 96 px
    of artwork inside 16 px of transparent padding, Chrome's convention for
    a 128 px icon. The same bytes as extension/icons/128.png. The 16, 32 and
    48 px package icons crop the padding away so the mark fills the toolbar.

screenshot-01-importing.png (1280x800, RGB)
    "Moves a claude.ai Project between your accounts." The command line and
    the side panel during an import: the job, the account, the spacing wait.

screenshot-02-stops-and-tells.png (1280x800, RGB)
    "Stops and tells you. Never retries on its own." A Cloudflare check
    held the import; the panel and the command line say what to do.

screenshot-03-done.png (1280x800, RGB)
    "Nothing to paste. Nothing leaves your computer but the project." The
    finished import with its counts and the new project's link.

promo-tile-440x280.png (440x280, RGB)
    The mark, the name and one line.

marquee-1400x560.png (1400x560, RGB)
    The first screenshot's headline beside the side panel.

The screenshots and tiles are 8-bit RGB with no alpha channel, as the store
asks (build_assets.mjs re-encodes Chromium's output and refuses a pixel that
is not opaque).
